1. PURPOSE
================================================================================

A single-page portfolio at rheanamindo.me that converts a visitor into remote,
asynchronous client work. It is not a CV rendered in a browser. The CV already
exists and carries the exhaustive detail; the site exists to make a stranger
want to write to Rheana.

Rheana is legally blind (850 myopia), so accessibility is a requirement of the
work rather than a score to chase.

================================================================================
2. STANDING RULES
================================================================================

These are rules Rheana has stated. They apply to every future change. A rule
here overrides any general preference.

R-01  Recommendations are VERBATIM.
      Other people's words about her are never edited, corrected, shortened,
      reordered or tidied. Not spelling, not grammar, not pronouns. They are
      also shown WHOLE - never clamped, scrolled or truncated.
      Guarded by: apps/web/test/proof.test.jsx
      VIOLATED ONCE on 2026-08-30 (grammar "fixed", text split into pull-quote
      plus remainder). Restored the same day.

R-02  The website is not the CV.
      No word vomit. One claim per section, one figure per claim. Everything
      else goes behind a disclosure, which keeps it in the HTML for crawlers
      while keeping it off the surface for readers.
      Guarded by: apps/web/test/work.test.jsx

R-03  Speak to the client, not about herself.
      Lead with their pain, answer as her. Second person first, first person
      second. Show value rather than asserting it. Some self-description is
      wanted - a byline is fine - but it never leads.

R-04  Every figure on the site must exist in the resume.
      work.json chooses which claim leads; resume.json is the source of every
      fact. No number is invented for the website.
      Guarded by: apps/web/test/work.test.jsx

R-05  No decorative images.
      Shapes and colour only, from the stated palette. The only images kept are
      the video resume, its poster and captions, and the RM monogram. The
      hydrangea mark is drawn in SVG, not an image.

R-06  No gingham, plaid or pattern grounds.
      Flat colour washes only.

R-07  English only for now.
      The i18n layer stays wired and Filipino strings stay maintained, but the
      language switcher is not shown.

R-08  Keep light, dark and auto themes. Keep text to speech.

R-09  Text-to-speech prefers a female Filipino voice, then female in the page
      language, then any female. Intelligibility outranks the preference.
      Guarded by: apps/web/test/pickVoice.test.js

R-10  ASCII where the character is a LETTER.
      resume -> resume, 61x not 61 multiplication-sign. But "n with tilde" in
      Los Banos stays: removing it misspells a real place name. Punctuation
      (dashes, middots) stays.

R-11  Do not link 2weeks.me. Sensitive subject.
      luxelips.com.au may be linked; an outbound link does not harm their SEO.

R-12  Never name Iosys internal repositories. The resume abstracts that work to
      "a C# ML platform" and the site keeps that abstraction.

R-13  Commits are single-line, type: description, lowercase, no Co-Authored-By
      and no session trailer. Per a1.standards/pr-review.standards.txt 4.1-4.2.

R-14  Motion is an invitation, not decoration. Everything animated must respect
      prefers-reduced-motion, and nothing may hide content permanently.
