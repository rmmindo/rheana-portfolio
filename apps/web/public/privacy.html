Privacy

Short version: this site does not set cookies, does not track you, and loads nothing from anyone else's servers.

  • No cookies are set. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • Fonts, video and captions are served from this site. No Google Fonts, no embedded YouTube, no third-party scripts.
  • The Baybayin playground runs entirely in your browser. What you type and what you draw never leave your device and are never stored.
  • Your theme choice and whether you have seen the intro are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only.
  • The connection is encrypted over HTTPS.

Who is responsible

Rheana Mindo, Philippines. You can reach me at rheanammindo@gmail.com.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time of the request, the file requested and your browser's user agent. That processing is what makes serving a web page possible at all, and those logs are kept and deleted by GitHub under their own policy. I do not have access to them.

Analytics

If visitor statistics are switched on for this site they are collected by GoatCounter, which sets no cookies, collects no personal data and does not track anyone across sites. It records that a page was viewed and roughly where in the world from. I cannot identify a visitor from it, and neither can it.

When you write to me

The contact form on the home page composes a message and sends it either by email or through a form service, depending on configuration. I use what you send only to answer you. I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not pass it to anyone. I keep the correspondence for as long as the conversation is live and delete it afterwards.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you have the right to be informed, to object, to access, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to damages, to data portability, and to file a complaint. Since almost nothing about you is collected here, most of these have little to act on, but they apply. An informal email is enough to exercise any of them.

Complaints can be made to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Automated decisions

There is no profiling and no automated decision-making on this site.
